## 1. Fluffling
**Base Form Name**: Fluffling  
**Element / Theme**: Fire / Playful Cloud  
**Resonance Affinity**: Joy (Primary) → Courage (Secondary)

### Visual Description
- **Base Form**: Tiny floating fiery cloud with big sparkling eyes and a wagging tail of flames. Looks like a marshmallow on fire.
- **Evolution 1**: Larger cloud with small wings and playful grin.
- **Evolution 2**: Full forms vary wildly by path.
- **Art Style Notes**: Warm oranges, yellows, soft glowing edges. Always expressive and bouncy.

### Personality & Lore
- Base personality: Cheerful, mischievous, easily excited.
- Loves attention and playing.
- Echo origin: Born from joyful memories of festivals in the old Digital Echo.

### Resonance Profile
| Axis       | Natural Lean | Training Style That Boosts It |
|------------|--------------|-------------------------------|
| Joy        | High         | Echo Dance, Bond Feast        |
| Discipline | Low          | Discipline Circuit            |
| Courage    | Medium       | Rift Exploration              |
| Harmony    | Low-Medium   | Harmony Garden                |

### Evolution Paths
1. **Prankster Inferno** (Joy + Courage)  
   - Fast trickster with firework attacks.
2. **Blazing Guardian** (Courage heavy)  
   - Noble knight-like form with flaming armor.
3. **Hearth Spirit** (Harmony + Joy)  
   - Warm, supportive fluffy protector.
4. **Prismflame** (Perfectly Balanced - Rare)  
   - Rainbow fire form with versatile abilities.

### Stats Growth Style
- High: Speed & Spirit  
- Medium: Strength  
- Low: Vitality

### Training Preferences
- Loves: Echo Dance, Bond Feast, Rift Exploration
- Does well in Echo Drift with Joy focus.
